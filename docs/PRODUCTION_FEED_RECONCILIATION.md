## Executive Summary

The real farm data shows a pattern different from the one the code review predicted.

1. **The per-animal feed cost shown in the app is overstated by ৳63,072 (+54%). [CONFIRMED]** The six cattle pages add up to **৳180,599** of feed. That is **more than all the feed the farm ever acquired (৳129,025)**, which is exactly the symptom reported. Reconstructed from the actual stock movements, the feed consumed is worth **৳117,527**.
2. **The ration quantity is not the cause.** The app's estimate (2,471.6 kg concentrate) is within **3%** of what the stock ledger actually shows as used (2,402.0 kg). The overstatement comes from:
   - **roughage kilograms priced at the per-piece straw price** (≈ ৳37,648);
   - **latest purchase prices used instead of the actual average cost** (৳23,622);
   - other smaller effects (৳1,802).
3. **89% of all stock movements (592 of 665) were generated by the automatic deduction engine.** Almost none were recorded by people. When physical stock ran out, 22 "True-Up" corrections totalling ৳22,041 absorbed the difference. That includes an 11-day stretch (09-07 → 09-17) when the engine never ran.
4. **Problems that are CONFIRMED in production:**
   - one day (2026-08-29) was deducted twice;
   - one item (Mix Feed) was consumed 34.38 kg beyond what was bought, while the app shows 0;
   - 38 consumption rows are valued at ৳0;
   - a 50 kg purchase was recorded at ৳0;
   - opening stock was recorded as a purchase;
   - straw is stocked in "pieces" while the app reasons in kg.
5. **Problems that did NOT occur in production:**
   - all 6 recipes have batch size = ingredient total (F-01 not triggered);
   - there is no feed mixing, no daily-button use, no feed sessions, no waste and no stock adjustments. The mixing and "adjustment as purchase" paths (F-02 mixing overlap, F-03 mixing, F-15) exist only in the code.
6. **Accounting [ESTIMATED]:** the accounting engine capitalizes about **৳184,314** of estimated feed into livestock, while ৳109,889 of the same feed is expensed. The balance-sheet feed inventory is forced to **৳0** instead of about **৳11,498**. No animal has been sold yet, so the double count is sitting in livestock value. It will reach profit and loss as COGS when animals are sold.

## Confirmed Root Causes (production evidence only)
1. **Estimated rations are stored as actual consumption** (592 auto rows), with true-ups absorbing the error. Physical daily usage is not recorded.
2. **Per-animal cost uses the latest purchase price and mixes units** (straw priced per piece, quantities in kg), while ignoring the farm's own pieces-per-day setting.
3. **The automatic engine is not idempotent:** a duplicated day, 15 missing days and 11 back-fill runs.
4. **Negative stock is hidden** (Mix Feed −34.38 kg shown as 0).
5. **Missing costs:** 38 consumption rows at ৳0, and a ৳0 purchase.
6. **Opening stock is recorded as a purchase.**
